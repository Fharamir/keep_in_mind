# Privacy Policy for Keep in Mind

*Last updated: November 2026 / Version 1.0.0*

**Keep in Mind** ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed as a **100% Local-First** productivity and self-sharing application. We believe strongly in user privacy, transparency, and data ownership.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Cloud
- **Keep in Mind does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data.**
- All content you create or share into the app—including notes, links, photos, audio recordings, documents, and tags—is stored **strictly and exclusively in your device's internal storage sandbox**.
- There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertisement trackers, and no external cloud servers connected to the app.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Usage

Keep in Mind requests only the minimum hardware permissions necessary to provide user-requested features:

- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**:
  - **Purpose**: Used solely when you tap the camera button inside the app to capture a photo or document attachment to store in your local memory library.
  - **Access**: Photos are processed and saved directly into the app's local private sandbox directory. The camera is never accessed in the background or without your explicit action.
- **Microphone / Record Audio (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)**:
  - **Purpose**: Used solely when you tap the microphone button inside the app to record a personal voice note.
  - **Access**: Audio files are recorded and stored directly into the app's local private sandbox directory. The microphone is never accessed in the background or without your explicit action.

Neither photos nor audio recordings are ever transmitted outside of your device by the app.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Backup, and Sharing
- **Local Storage**: All files and database entries reside in the app's private internal storage directory managed by the Android operating system.
- **Export & Backup**: When you choose to export your library via JSON backup, the resulting backup file is handled entirely under your control using your device's native file picker or share sheet.
- **Share to Self**: When you share content to Keep in Mind from other apps (e.g., browsers, social media, gallery), the content is passed directly via Android's local Intent system into Keep in Mind's local storage.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Links
- Keep in Mind contains **no third-party tracking, profiling, advertising, or cloud analytics libraries**.
- If you save web links in Keep in Mind and choose to tap on them, they will open in your default external web browser according to your system settings. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Keep in Mind does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of Keep in Mind, changes will be posted in this document and reflected in future app updates.
